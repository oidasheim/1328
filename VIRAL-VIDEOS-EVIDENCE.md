# Short-form engagement evidence profile

This repository's creative heuristics are planning aids only. They do not predict
virality, estimate returns, or claim knowledge of platform ranking formulas.

## Evidence synthesis

- Emotional arousal has been associated with engagement and sharing in empirical
  work, but effect size and direction vary by context and platform.
- First-seconds hooks and rhetorical devices are empirically associated with
  attention, yet category norms and the complete algorithmic context matter.
- Watch time, completion and interactions are observable platform metrics. They
  are correlated engagement signals, not guarantees of distribution or success.
- Cross-platform behavior should be treated as a research hypothesis until
  measured for the user's own channel and audience.

## Responsible planning rules

- Do not use fear, shame or false urgency merely to increase engagement.
- Prefer surprise, humor, practical value and truthful local context.
- Publish a clear hook and a readable payoff; then measure real outcomes.
- Use A/B tests to test candidates, not as certainty of success.
- Keep human approval, rights verification and platform/account compliance as
  mandatory publication gates.
- Keep `EPHEMERAL predictions` separate from `REAL telemetry`. Forecast values
  must never become platform ground truth.
